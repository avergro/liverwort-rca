## Table S10b. Gene Ontology term sets defining each functional context

Sets are those that define the Gene Ontology functional contexts. Annotations were propagated to ancestor terms through is_a, part_of and the three regulates relations, so a gene annotated to a descendant of a listed term counts for that context. Primary metabolism is the union of the carbon, nitrogen, lipid, nucleotide and cofactor sets. The regulatory set excludes GO:0006355 and GO:0007165, which alone covered about twice the genes of carbon metabolism.
