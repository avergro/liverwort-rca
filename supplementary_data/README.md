# Dataset S1

Result tables that support the manuscript and Supplementary Note S1 but are not printed in the supplementary file. All files are tab-separated and are regenerated by `22_build_supplementary.py`.

- `bootstrap_liverworts_top_two.tsv`: Fraction of bootstrap replicates with both liverworts first and second. Bootstrap over orthogroups, KEGG contexts.
- `dated_topology_vcv.tsv`: Variance-covariance matrix of the dated topology of Table S1. Shared root-to-tip path length between two species divided by the total tree height.
- `eigenvalue_spectrum.tsv`: Eigenvalue spectrum and correspondence analysis inertia. Eigenvalues of the species-by-species matrix T of equation 2 in the main text (the first, equal to one, is omitted) and the share of inertia carried by the first two axes of a correspondence analysis of the same binary matrix.
- `estimator_ranks.tsv`: Rank of each species under three estimators in each context, given as RCA score / RCA diversity / Fitness-Complexity (S / D / F), ordered by the whole-genome rank of the RCA score.
- `go_kegg_overlap.tsv`: Overlap between the KEGG and Gene Ontology orthogroup sets of each context. Orthogroups assigned to each context by the two annotations, before the RCA filter.
- `go_term_sets.tsv`: Gene Ontology term sets defining each functional context. Sets are those that define the Gene Ontology functional contexts. Annotations were propagated to ancestor terms through is_a, part_of and the three regulates relations, so a gene annotated to a descendant of a listed term counts for that context. Primary metabolism is the union of the carbon, nitrogen, lipid, nucleotide and cofactor sets. The regulatory set excludes GO:0006355 and GO:0007165, which alone covered about twice the genes of carbon metabolism.
- `kegg_module_step_fractions.tsv`: Fraction of the steps of each KEGG module satisfied in each species. Modules complete in at least one land plant (Table S5).
- `kegg_pathway_sets.tsv`: KEGG pathway sets defining each functional context. Sets are those that define the KEGG functional contexts; primary metabolism is the union of all metabolic sets, and ubiquinone biosynthesis, listed under both lipids and cofactors, is counted once. The equivalent Gene Ontology term sets are listed in go_term_sets.tsv.
- `liverwort_enriched_orthogroups.tsv`: Liverwort-enriched orthogroups with their copy numbers, KEGG pathways and gene identifiers. Orthogroups with RCA ≥ 1 in both liverworts and RCA < 1 in all three angiosperms (Table S7).
- `liverwort_enriched_pathways.tsv`: Pathway composition of the liverwort-enriched orthogroups, all pathways with at least three. Columns as in Table S7 of the supplementary material.
- `nestedness.tsv`: Nestedness against the null model. NODF computed exactly by grouping orthogroups into binary patterns.
- `null_model.tsv`: Degree-preserving null model, all species and contexts. z scores are reported for reference; the standard deviations of some null distributions are very small and the corresponding z values should not be interpreted as effect sizes.
- `pgls_fits.tsv`: Phylogenetic regression, all fits. Phylogenetic generalized least squares regression of the RCA score of each context on the natural logarithm of gene number, alone or with BUSCO completeness, on the dated topology of Table S1. Pagel λ is estimated by maximum likelihood between 0 and 1. The log-likelihood (logL) is computed with the residual variance estimated with n − p degrees of freedom, which shifts every profile by a constant and leaves the estimate of λ unchanged.
- `pgls_residual_ranks.tsv`: Residual rank of each species in the phylogenetic regression. Rank of the residual of each species (1, largest positive residual) in the regression of the RCA score on the natural logarithm of gene number on the dated topology of Table S1. In parentheses, the range of ranks across the settings of pgls_sensitivity.tsv (without the alga, root at 750, 1,000 and 1,500 million years, and every land plant node at the younger or at the older bound of its interval); no parentheses when the rank did not change.
- `pgls_sensitivity.tsv`: Phylogenetic regression, sensitivity to the outgroup and the root age. The regression of pgls_fits.tsv repeated without the alga, with the root of the topology at 750, 1,000 and 1,500 million years, and with every land plant node at the younger or at the older bound of the interval given by its source in Table S1.
- `rarefaction.tsv`: Proportion of rarefaction replicates with both liverworts in the first two ranks, by subsampling depth (genes per proteome). The four depths below 12,702 subsample every proteome below the smallest one.
- `rarefaction_symmetric.tsv`: Rarefaction below the smallest proteome. Fraction of replicates with both liverworts first and second and their mean ranks, at depths where all eleven proteomes are subsampled.
- `rna_modification_orthogroups.tsv`: The 25 conserved orthogroups annotated to RNA modification and absent from both liverworts. Gene numbers per species, whether the *Arabidopsis* members include a pentatricopeptide repeat (PPR) protein, and the evidence codes of the direct annotations of those members to RNA modification or its descendant terms (IEA, inferred from electronic annotation; IBA, inferred from biological aspect of ancestor).
- `sign_rule.tsv`: Orientation of the axis under the two sign rules. Spearman correlation between the orderings given by the rule of the main text (positive correlation with RCA diversity) and by the largest-component rule, for each context and RCA threshold.
- `simulation_grid.tsv`: Simulation grid. Recovery is the partial Spearman correlation with the planted capability endowment, controlling for gene number.
- `simulation_grid_40_species.tsv`: Simulation grid with 40 species. As simulation_grid.tsv, with 40 species.
- `simulation_recovery_summary.tsv`: Recovery of the simulated capability endowment, as partial Spearman correlation controlling for gene number, with eleven species and heterogeneous endowment.
- `simulation_relaxations.tsv`: Relaxations of the simulation model. Recovery under each relaxation of the capability model, given as the partial Spearman correlation with the planted endowment, controlling for gene number, with its sign. Scores are oriented by RCA diversity, as in the main text; simulation_grid.tsv reports both the signed and the absolute value.
